WORK INSTRUCTION · MWL-TEST-001 · REV H

Functional Test Procedure

Purpose

Verify ET 200SP modules, pneumatic valves, electrical feedback, valve-position feedback, and connected sensors using PRONETA Basic.

Setup

Confirm RIO A and RIO B online, correct module parameters, verified RTD wiring, and approved air supply. Isolate the process and clear the valve travel area.

In PRONETA: IO Test → Device Selection → select station → Force and Monitor Values (only when safe). Use Test Results and Diagnostics.

Before commanding an output

Test one valve at a time. Do not energize opposing OPEN/CLOSE coils together. Isolate power and air before wiring changes; do not alter Ex or safety circuits.

Functional requirements

Modules

Online, powered, no unexplained active fault.

Valves

Correct valve moves in both directions twice.

Electrical feedback

_fb responds to its solenoid command.

Valve position

-pos follows actual travel.

Sensors

Input responds to an approved physical change and returns.

Restoration

Equipment safe and forcing disabled.

Procedure

01
Verify modules

Confirm each station and module is online. Check Diagnostics and record the status.

02
Test valve and feedback

Command each DQ. Confirm valve travel, matching _fb and -pos. Return and repeat one more cycle.

03
Test RTD response

Record ambient reading. Gently warm sensing area, then cool; record all three readings.

04
Test PD / DP response

Record baseline. Apply pressure only after confirming safe rated limits. Record changed and return readings; otherwise mark Pending.

05
Restore and save

Return valves to safe state, turn forcing OFF, and download the current skid results.

Reading results

Pass: expected observed response. Fail: incorrect response. Pending: test cannot safely be completed. Not tested: not started.

_fb verifies electrical feedback; -pos follows physical valve movement. Neither a DQ LED nor _fb alone proves valve travel. Baseline readings alone do not prove sensor response.

PD pressure span and DP specifications require confirmation before applied-pressure testing.
